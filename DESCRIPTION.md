# Sendcloud

Connect your Swell store to Sendcloud to send orders there automatically and get tracking back.

- **Automatic order sync:** paid orders (or submitted ones, if you choose) are sent to Sendcloud's Incoming Orders with the shipping address, items, weights and totals, ready for you to create labels.
- **Tracking in Swell:** Sendcloud's webhook keeps each order's parcel status, carrier, tracking number and tracking link up to date on a Sendcloud tab on the order.
- **Automatic fulfillment:** when a label is created in Sendcloud, the order is fulfilled in Swell with the carrier, shipping method and tracking number.
- **Cancellations:** canceled Swell orders are removed from Sendcloud's Incoming Orders, and canceled parcels cancel the matching Swell shipment.
- **Simple setup:** add your Sendcloud API keys and integration ID, then paste the webhook URL into your Sendcloud integration.
